# Overview

COGS 108 - Data Science in Practice - is a class offered by the Cognitive Science Department of UC San Diego, taught by Professors Shannon Ellis, Jason Fleischer, and Bradley Voytek. Here is an overview and map of the COGS 108 Organization, which hosts materials and assignments for the class.

## Syllabus 

The most recent iteration of this class is Fall 2026 quarter, the **syllabus** for which is available [here](https://github.com/COGS108/Overview/blob/fa26/COGS108-Syllabus.md).


## Lectures

[Slides and materials](https://github.com/COGS108/Lectures-Ellis/tree/fa26) will be organized by week. All links to class videos, slides, and notebooks used MWF will be included here.

## Discussion Section

Discussion will be dedicated to project completion and discussion of data science readings this term.

## Assignments

Assignments will be completed on [datahub.ucsd.edu](http://datahub.ucsd.edu).

## Readings & Resources

- A suggested [reading list](https://github.com/COGS108/Readings) (recommended, but not required).
- Helpful [resources](https://github.com/COGS108/Resources) to support your learning or dig deeper into topics taught (to be used as needed/interested).



## Final Projects

A core component of the class is completing a group project. Details about the final project can be found [here](https://github.com/COGS108/Projects/tree/fa26).

---

## A reminder about time: 

Assignments are intermingled with your project proposal, checkpoints and final project. This is an assignment- and project-heavy course load to get you as much practice as possible. This will require good time management and planning on your part. Start planning ahead now to avoid late submissions and issues later in the quarter.
